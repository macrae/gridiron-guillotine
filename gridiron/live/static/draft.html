what the numbers mean
VORP
projected points above a replacement-level starter at that position. Comparable across positions — that is the whole point of it. A 176-VORP RB and a 176-VORP WR are worth the same to your lineup.
score
VORP adjusted for what your roster still needs and how likely the player is to last until your next pick. This is what the ranking sorts on.
cliff
the VORP you give up by waiting: best available at that position now, minus the best you would expect when you next pick. A big cliff is why a 76-VORP TE can beat three 90-VORP RBs.
urg
score minus VORP — how much of the ranking is urgency rather than raw value.
% to last
chance he is still on the board at your next pick.
ADP
average draft position: where the room is expected to take him. Value sits where ADP is far later than VORP rank.
left
how many draftable players remain at that position.
●
research sentiment — green nothing flagged, amber a one-to-two game concern or role risk, red multi-week absence. Green is silence in the feeds, not a clean bill of health.
