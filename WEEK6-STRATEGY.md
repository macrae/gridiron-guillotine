## Lessons applied (from this season's notes)

1. **Q player + early kickoff + similar backup in a later game → swap in advance.** Doesn't apply this week: Olave and Warren both play at 1pm, and their backups play the same time or later, so we can wait for the 11:30 ET inactives.
2. **Don't over-manage the margins** (Week 3: both losses came from fiddling). Every move below fixes a zero or gains 2+ points. No coin-flip stacking.
3. **After a free-agent add, check the slot actually filled.** Yahoo puts added players on the bench.
4. **Reload the roster page between lineup swaps.**
5. **File Wednesday claims by Tuesday night.**
6. **Judge players on merit, not as contingencies.** Golden has outscored the player starting over him twice (Wk1 15.5, Wk3 21+, and Wk5 20.3 in FD).
